SUNDIAL
Timestamp Converter
How to Use
Step 1 - Select Text
Highlight a timestamp number on screen (in terminal, log file, browser, etc.)
Step 2 - Press Hotkey
Press F9 (or your custom hotkey). Sundial will copy the selected text automatically.
Step 3 - See Result
A popup appears near your cursor showing the converted time. Click to copy!
Features
Auto Copy: Hotkey simulates Ctrl+C for you
Clipboard Restore: Original clipboard is preserved
Timezone Support: Convert to any timezone
Customizable Hotkey: F6-F12 available
Tray Menu
Convert (F9) - Manual trigger
Settings... - Change hotkey & timezone
Help - This window
Quit - Exit completely
